## Research focus

- **Bot detection & hunting:** behavioral fingerprints, coordinated activity, and the signals that distinguish automated behavior from human activity.
- **Adversarial AI:** prompt injection, model manipulation, tool abuse, and trust boundaries in AI agents.
- **Software & intelligent systems:** practical applications of AI, explainable decisions, and tools that help people understand complex systems.

My approach: stay curious, test assumptions, and document what I learn.
